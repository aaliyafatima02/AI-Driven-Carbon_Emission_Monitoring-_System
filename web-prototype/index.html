            Platform Overview & Architecture
            
About CarbonIQ

            

              CarbonIQ is an environmental intelligence and decision-support platform designed to monitor carbon emissions and model optimal reduction pathways for regional infrastructure planning.
            

          


          

            
Platform Mission

            

              Empowering Regional Decarbonization with Quantitative Analytics
            

            

              Managing carbon footprints across complex economic sectors requires more than raw data tracking—it demands systematic analysis of spatial relationships, corridor infrastructure costs, and capital-efficient abatement investments.
            

            

              CarbonIQ provides environmental planners, facility managers, and sustainability teams with clear situational awareness of their emissions inventory and deterministic decision models to plan capital-effective reduction programs.
            

          


          

            

              

                Spatial Graph Topology
              

              

                Monitored sectors are modeled as continuous emission vertices connected by weighted corridors representing sensor telemetry links, transport corridors, and infrastructure conduits. This enables spatial route optimization and minimum-cost telemetry backbone design.
              

            


            

              

                Combinatorial Abatement Optimization
              

              

                Rather than relying on heuristic guesswork, CarbonIQ employs dynamic programming models to select the most cost-effective portfolio of non-divisible decarbonization interventions within any capital budget ceiling.
              

            


            

              

                Zero-Dependency Architecture
              

              

                Built with modern web standards (HTML5, CSS3, vanilla ES6+ JavaScript), CarbonIQ operates entirely client-side with zero external library bloat. It provides immediate responsiveness, maximum security, and full offline accessibility.
              

            

          


          

            

              

                Dataset & Operational Transparency
              

              

                

                  Current platform analytics operate on a verified baseline dataset covering six economic sectors (Power Plant, Industrial Area, Transport Hub, Commercial Zone, Residential Area, and Green Zone) and five candidate engineering interventions.
                

                

                  The platform does not claim real-time continuous IoT hardware streaming or trained machine-learning predictions at this stage. It is designed as an algorithmic decision-support framework that can be connected to live sensor networks in future phases.
